KNOW WHAT YOU’RE SEEING

A little context goes a long way.

What confirms a trade?

Fresh, matching research must pass sample, payout coverage, expected value and risk checks.

Why is a strategy waiting?

It may need more samples, fresh market data, or an exact research adapter. Unsupported strategies stay blocked while confirmation is enabled.

What does the score mean?

An index combining expected value, drawdown, losing streak and data coverage. A score of 80 is not an 80% chance of winning.

Are these live trading profits?

No. These are paper observations using a one-unit stake. Unpriced results have no assumed payout. Actual execution and future results can differ.
